# Mike Beverly Website — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Audience: Integrative, functional, naturopathic, chiropractic, and hands-on practitioners.
Goal: Establish credibility, communicate the 29-Day Healing Gap concept, and convert practitioners to book a briefing.
Tone: Serious, credible, human, evidence-honest. NOT salesy, NOT clinical-cold.

## Idea A — "Clinical Authority" (Editorial Dark)

**Design Movement:** Dark editorial meets scientific publication — think Nature journal meets premium health consultancy.

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep navy base with surgical white text — authority without sterility
2. Teal as the single accent colour — clinical precision, not corporate blue
3. Generous whitespace with tight typographic hierarchy
4. Data and evidence presented like a well-designed research brief

**Color Philosophy:** Navy (#0F1E38) base signals trust and depth. Teal (#0E7C7B) is the "signal" colour — used only for CTAs, numbers, and key phrases. Warm amber (#F59E0B) used sparingly for the "29 days" number to create urgency without alarm.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial — full-bleed dark sections alternating with light content panels. Hero uses left-aligned text with large typographic number "29" as a visual anchor. No centered hero layouts.

**Signature Elements:**
- The "29" as a recurring visual motif — large, amber, typographic
- Horizontal teal rule lines as section dividers
- Pull-quote blocks with left teal border (matching the PDF style)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth scroll-triggered fade-ins. Hover states on cards reveal teal border. CTA buttons use fill-on-hover animation.

**Animation:** Subtle entrance animations — text slides up 20px on scroll entry. Numbers count up when in viewport. No excessive motion.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display Bold — gravitas and editorial authority
- Body: Inter Regular/Medium — clean, readable, clinical
- Accent labels: Space Grotesk — technical, modern

## Idea B — "Human Science" (Warm Dark)

**Design Movement:** Premium health brand meets personal memoir — warm, human, credible. Like a well-produced documentary.

**Core Principles:**
1. Warm dark backgrounds (deep charcoal-navy) rather than cold navy
2. Typography does the heavy lifting — large, expressive headlines
3. Photography-forward — the practitioner image is central to every key section
4. Story-first structure — the science emerges from the human narrative

**Color Philosophy:** Charcoal (#1A1F2E) base feels warmer than pure navy. Teal (#14A8A7) for accents. Warm cream (#F5F0E8) for light sections — not pure white, which feels clinical. Amber for the "29" motif.

**Layout Paradigm:** Magazine-style — wide image panels on alternating sides. Text never centered; always left or right aligned to create visual tension and movement.

**Signature Elements:**
- Warm cream panels between dark sections for rhythm
- Large italic pull-quotes in Playfair Display
- Subtle grain texture overlay on dark sections for depth

**Interaction Philosophy:** Parallax scroll on hero image. Cards lift slightly on hover. Smooth page transitions between routes.

**Animation:** Hero text reveals word-by-word on load. Section images fade in with slight scale-up. Staggered card entrances.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display Bold Italic — personal, authoritative, editorial
- Body: Source Serif Pro — warm, readable, not sterile

## Selected Approach: Idea A — "Clinical Authority" (Dark Editorial)

**Rationale:** The dark editorial approach best matches the existing brand assets (the PDF, the social graphics) and the audience psychology. Practitioners respond to authority and credibility signals. The deep navy + teal palette is already established. The asymmetric editorial layout avoids the "AI slop" centered layout trap. Playfair Display adds gravitas without being cold.

## Style Decisions

- Blog and article routes use a restrained amber **29** as a recurring typographic anchor. Amber remains reserved for the 29-Day Healing Gap and high-value urgency moments.
- Long-form article pages use teal rules and evidence-style callouts to create a clinical-publication rhythm without weakening reading comfort.
- The blog archive treats the first secondary story as a larger editorial feature, creating a clearer issue-like hierarchy than a uniform card grid.
